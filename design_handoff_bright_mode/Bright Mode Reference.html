Bright Mode — lighter, airier, the same soul

The plum-and-lavender identity, re-lit for daylight. Surfaces lift toward white, text drops to a soft deep-plum, and accents stay bold but never sit on a same-value background. This page is the visual contract — every panel below is rendered with the proposed .light token values.

Adjacency rule: a colored fill never touches a same-value surface. Pale accents (lavender, pink-soft, accent-light, success-bright) are fill-only on Bright — never used as text, because they vanish on white. When an accent must be text on a light tint, use its darkened ramp (below).
